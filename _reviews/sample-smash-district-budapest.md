The edges on these patties are properly crispy, with that dark, almost-burnt crust you only get from a screaming-hot griddle and a heavy press. Two thin patties, American cheese melted into every gap, pickled red onions cutting through the fat.

## The bun

Soft potato roll, toasted on the cut side. It held together to the last bite, which is more than most places manage.

## Would I go back?

Already have. Twice.
