# About Bun Voyage

I eat a lot of burgers. Also ribs, steak and anything else that's been near a grill. This is where I write down what was worth it and what wasn't, wherever in the world I am.

## How the scoring works

Every place gets an **overall score out of 10**. Most reviews also have a breakdown. For burgers that's usually:

- **Patty**: flavour, seasoning, cook, juiciness
- **Bun**: does it hold together, is it toasted, does it taste like anything
- **Toppings**: balance, quality, sauce
- **Value**: what you get for what you pay

Ribs and steak get their own categories (bark, tenderness, smoke, sides…). The overall score isn't an average. It's how I felt walking out.

**What the numbers mean:** 9+ is worth planning a trip around. 8 means go back. 7 is solid. 6 is fine. Anything below 5 I'd skip.
